2 days tour from Agadir to Sahara Desert

Introduction

Feel the pulse of the Sahara Desert on this once-in-a-lifetime adventure, which begins and ends in Agadir.

Admire the best landscapes of the northern Sahara, get lost in the desert and ride a camel through the dunes at sunset, like the nomadic Berber tribes. This desert exploration offers fun overnight camp nights and desert kasbahs.

Day 1: Agadir - Taroudant - Taliouine - Foum Zguid - Erg Chegaga

Early morning departure from Agadir to Taliouine, a city known for its saffron, with a stop en route in Taroudant to enjoy its ancient ramparts and its souk. After a few hours of travel, we will arrive in Taznakht for lunch and then through the Zguid Forum, we will head to the huge sand dunes of Erg Chegaga. Here you can watch a picturesque sunset, take a 1-hour camel ride before dinner, and spend the night in nomadic tents or starlight in a bivouac.

Day 2: Erg Chi gaga - Lake Iriqui - Taliouine - Taroudante - Agadir

Enjoy the sunrise and breakfast before setting off in a 4 × 4 vehicle to Foum Zguide via the dry lake of Iriqui. After resting in Foum Zguid, we go to Taznakht then to Taliouine, where lunch will be served. We then continue our journey back to Agadir and arrive in the city at sunset.

What is included

Air-conditioned tourist 4 WD, Gasoline.

Pick up & Drop off at your hotel.

English speaking tour guide, driver.

2 nights stay in desert tent and hotel (includes dinner and breakfast).

Camel ride.

Multiple stops to visit kasbahs en route.

Lunch

Drinks
