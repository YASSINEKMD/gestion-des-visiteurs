Middle Atlas & Desert From Fes to Marrakech

Middle Atlas & Desert From Fes to Marrakech

  • From 930.00 €
  • 4 Days / 3 Nights
Add to cart

FES – Ifrane – Ziz Valley – Erfoud – Merzouga – Jorf – Todra Gorges – Roses Valley – Skoura – Ouarzazate – Aït Benhaddou – MARRAKECH

Day 1: Fes – Ifrane – Azrou – Midelt – Errachidia – Ziz valley – Erfoud – Merzouga
KM = 470 – TIME = 7H00

After breakfast, you travel through Mid Atlas Mountains with changing landscape and changing culture toward the desert of Merzouga. We pass through the cedar woods of the middle Atlas, passing by Ifrane, a charming ski resort and Azrou an important handicraft center specialized in the work of cedar wood. After the Zad pass (2,178m/7,146ft) you arrive in Midelt for lunch. Afternoon you will drive towards Erfoud via Errachidia and the Ziz valley dotted with palm trees. Continuation to the sand dunes of Erg Chebbi. Dinner and over night in desert camp.

DAY 2 – Erg Chebbi
Day 1: Day in the desert

Live a full experience desert day.
After breakfast, camels are waiting for you for a trek in the red dunes of Erg Chebbi. With your experienced cameleer you will enjoy the serenity and silence of the desert.
Before noon, we will leave in 4x4 for an excursion in the desert with lunch.
2ND night in your luxury desert camp.

Day 3: Merzouga – Erfoud -Todra gorges –Dades gorges- Roses valley – Skoura
KM = 323 – TIME = 4H30

Early morning before sunrise, return by camels to Merzouga. Departure to Rissani and Erfoud in order to visit the treatment plant marble fossilized before continuing Tinjdad palm groves. Arrival in Todra Gorges for lunch. In the afternoon you will drive to Dades gorges via Boumalne and the Dades valley. The track road crosses Roses valley with its kasbah and ksours, beautiful nice landscapes before getting to the huge palmgrove of Skoura before sunset (about 40 min drive). Dinner and night in a charming guesthouse in the palmgrove.

Day 4: Skoura – Marrakech
KM = 238 – TIME = 4H15

After a nice homemade breakfast, we will leave the beautiful palm grove to reach Ouarzazate (40 min drive). Enjoy a full visit of Ouarzazate with the Movie Museum, Taourirt Kasbah (built in XVII century by the powerful Glaoui tribe), or the famous cinema studios.  
We will leave Ouarzazate after lunch to take the road to Marrakech, passing through Tichka Mountain pass (2,260 m) before arriving at the end of the day.
