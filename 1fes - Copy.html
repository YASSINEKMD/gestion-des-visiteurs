Day 1: Tangier - Chefchaouen

This private tour will take you to one of Africa’s most impressive waterfalls, Ouzoud Falls. Since you will be traveling on your own with your private party, you will always be in control, with the flexibility to stop whenever you want to take photos or just relax. Pickup from your hotel in Marrakech, you can enjoy the sights along the route in the comfort of a private, air-conditioned vehicle. You will also be accompanied by a local English-speaking Moroccan guide and driver who knows the region very well and will be able to answer all your questions. You will have time for lunch at the falls, and while the cost of food is not included, your guide will be happy to recommend a good local restaurant. Besides the falls, you will also stop at the ImiIfri natural bridge. The tour ends in Marrakech, when you are dropped off at your hotel.
